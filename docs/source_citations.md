# Source citations for dataset inventory

Use the official accession pages in the manuscript and README.

- GSE221156: GEO accession page, human pancreatic islet 10x scRNA-seq atlas; 245,878 cells, 48 donors, 17 ND, 14 PD, 17 T2D.
- GSE153855: GEO accession page, single-cell RNA-seq of human pancreatic islets from control and T2D donors; 6 control and 5 T2D donors.
- E-MTAB-5061: EBI Single Cell Expression Atlas/HCA, single-cell RNA-seq analysis of human pancreas from healthy individuals and T2D patients; 2,942 cells.
- GSE81608: GEO accession page, 1,492 human pancreatic alpha, beta, delta and PP cells from ND and T2D donors.
- GSE86469: GEO accession page, 638 human pancreatic islet/other single cells from 5 ND and 3 T2D donors.
- GSE83139: GEO accession page, endocrine cells from juvenile, adult control, T1D and T2D donors.
- GSE164416: GEO accession page, whole-islet RNA-seq from 133 donors: 18 ND, 41 IGT, 35 T3cD and 39 T2D.
